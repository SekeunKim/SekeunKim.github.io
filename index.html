Biography

I am Sekeun Kim (김세근), an AI researcher working on medical imaging and cardiovascular medicine, currently an Instructor / Faculty Member in the Department of Radiology at Harvard Medical School. My research develops AI methodologies for medical imaging and applies them to real clinical problems.

My research focuses on medical image computing, multimodal AI, and medical foundation models. I develop methods that learn from medical images and complementary clinical data to support disease detection, quantitative assessment, and clinical decision-making. My work spans self-supervised representation learning, image and video analysis, and the adaptation of foundation models to medical applications.

Cardiovascular medicine is a central application area of my research, connecting methodological advances with clinically meaningful questions about cardiac function, vascular disease, and patient risk. Working closely with clinical collaborators, I investigate how AI can integrate information across imaging modalities and clinical data sources to provide reliable, useful insights across diverse patients and care settings.

My broader goal is to develop practical, trustworthy AI that can be integrated into everyday care. I am particularly interested in technologies that reduce the cost of delivering health and social care services by easing clinical workloads and helping providers use limited resources more effectively. Through this work, I aim to make high-quality care more affordable and accessible while preserving patient safety and quality of care.
